# LocalioBC 1.3.0

LocalioBC adds optional direct uploads of finished exports to your own Bunny Storage account. Editing and exporting remain local and account-free.

- Upload one merged video or a batch of separate clips after reviewing the files and destination.
- Configure Storage in Settings; passwords are stored in macOS Keychain.
- Track, cancel and retry transfers, then copy or open CDN links.
- Restore interrupted jobs for an explicit retry after reopening the app.
- Use the clean Czech/English interface, including the updated start screen and compact transfer list.

Bunny.net is a separately paid service; it is not included with the free LocalioBC app. Keep the app open during uploads. HTTP retries restart the file.

The direct edition is free and open source, without subscriptions or watermarks. Universal for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or later.
