# LocalioBC 1.2.1

- **Cleaner export panel:** controls share one surface, grouped with whitespace and typography, without decorative frames, tinted section backgrounds or separator lines.
- **No sidebar scrolling:** all export options and the main action fit at the default and supported minimum window sizes.
- Quality, target-size and lossless modes, merged and separate exports, progress, cancellation and result actions remain available.
- The approved design and native layout checks are recorded in the project rules to preserve the layout in future changes.

Video editing and export still work offline. The Mac App Store edition receives its updates through Apple.
