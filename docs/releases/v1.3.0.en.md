# LocalioBC 1.3.0

## Upload finished videos to Bunny Storage

- Upload a merged video or individually exported clips directly to your own Bunny Storage account.
- Set up the Storage Zone, region, destination folder and CDN address in Settings. Your password stays in macOS Keychain.
- Review the finished files before starting a transfer. Follow progress, cancel or retry, and copy or open the resulting CDN links.
- The transfer queue is independent of the editor and remembers interrupted jobs for an explicit retry after relaunch. Keep the app open during uploads; HTTP retries restart the file.
- The start screen introduces Bunny Storage with a compact affiliate signup link and a link to Localio Labs.

Editing and export continue to work locally without an account. The full direct-download edition remains free and open source. Bunny.net is a separately paid service; it is not included with the free LocalioBC app.

This release also preserves the compact export controls, continuous speed slider, batch crop, audio removal and merged/separate export modes. Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
