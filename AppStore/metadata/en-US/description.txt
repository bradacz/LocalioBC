LocalioBC is a simple, quick way to crop video on your Mac. Open your clips, choose the frame and export. Clear controls keep everyday video edits straightforward.

CROP IN A FEW STEPS
Choose a familiar aspect ratio — 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 4:3 — or adjust a custom frame. Apply the crop to one clip or use the same crop across all your clips. Preview the result before exporting.

TRIM AND EXPORT
Shorten the beginning or end using a thumbnail timeline. Reorder and merge clips into one video, or export each clip as a separate numbered file. Remove audio when you only need the picture.

CONTROL FILE SIZE
Export MP4 or MOV with H.264 or HEVC. Choose a quality preset, set a resolution limit or enter an estimated target size. Eligible clips can use lossless export without changing the image.

LOCAL AND PRIVATE
Your video and audio stay on your Mac. Editing and export work offline, with no account, uploads, analytics, ads or watermarks.

MADE FOR MAC
A native interface with light, dark and system appearance. Czech is selected automatically on Czech systems; other system languages use English. You can always choose the language in Settings.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Target sizes are estimates; lossless trimming can depend on source keyframes. HEVC availability depends on your Mac. Project save/load is not included.
