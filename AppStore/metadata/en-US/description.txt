LocalioBC is a simple, quick way to crop video on your Mac. Open your clips, choose the frame and export. Clear controls keep everyday video edits straightforward.

CROP IN A FEW STEPS
Choose a familiar aspect ratio — 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 4:3 — or adjust a custom frame. Apply the crop to one clip or use the same crop across all your clips. Preview the result before exporting.

TRIM AND EXPORT
Shorten the beginning or end using a thumbnail timeline. Reorder and merge clips into one video, or export each clip as a separate numbered file. Remove audio when you only need the picture.

SET THE PACE
Adjust each clip smoothly from 0.25× to 4×, check the exact speed and reset to 1× with one click. Preview and export use the same speed; audio pitch changes with speed.

CONTROL FILE SIZE
Export MP4 or MOV with H.264 or HEVC. Choose a quality preset, set a resolution limit or enter an estimated target size. Eligible clips can use lossless export without changing the image.

OPTIONAL BUNNY STORAGE UPLOADS
Send finished exports to your own Bunny Storage account. Review the files, sizes and destination before starting an upload. Follow progress, cancel or retry transfers, and copy or open completed CDN links. This works with merged videos and separate clip exports.

Bunny Storage is a separate, optional paid service. Its storage and CDN charges are billed to your own Bunny.net account; LocalioBC does not add an upload fee. A clearly marked affiliate signup link is available, and signup is not required to edit or export videos.

LOCAL BY DEFAULT
Import, editing, previews and export work offline without an account. Nothing uploads automatically. Optional transfers go directly over HTTPS to your configured Bunny Storage account, without passing through Localio Labs servers. The Storage Zone password stays in macOS Keychain; connection settings and the transfer queue are stored locally. There are no analytics or watermarks.

MADE FOR MAC
A native interface with light, dark and system appearance. Czech is selected automatically on Czech systems; other system languages use English. You can always choose the language in Settings.

One-time purchase. No subscription or in-app purchases. LocalioBC is open source under the MIT license.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Target sizes are estimates; lossless trimming can depend on source keyframes. HEVC availability depends on your Mac. Project save/load is not included.
