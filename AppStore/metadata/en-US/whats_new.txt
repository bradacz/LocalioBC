Version 1.3.0 adds optional uploads of finished videos to your own Bunny Storage account.

• Configure your Storage Zone, region, destination folder and CDN address in Settings. The password is stored in macOS Keychain.
• Review merged or separate clip exports before explicitly starting an HTTPS upload.
• Follow a persistent transfer queue, cancel or retry uploads, and copy or open completed CDN links.
• Import, crop, trim, speed adjustment, audio removal and local export remain available offline without a Bunny account.

Bunny Storage is a separate paid service billed to your own account. The app clearly identifies its optional affiliate signup link.
