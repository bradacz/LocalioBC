LocalioBC 1.3.0: from a finished export to Bunny Storage

November 15, 2026 · The Localio Labs team

Prepare a smaller video on macOS, then upload the finished file directly to your own Bunny Storage account. LocalioBC 1.3.0 connects the last step of editing with the first step of publishing.

Trim, crop, compress — then upload

The free, open-source direct edition still gives you quick video trimming, frame cropping, file-size reduction and audio removal without ads or watermarks. The new optional upload action works with one merged video or a batch of separately exported clips.

  1. Connect your storage. In Settings → Bunny Storage, enter your Storage Zone, primary region, destination folder and connected Pull Zone/CDN HTTPS address. Use the Storage Zone password; it stays in macOS Keychain.
  2. Review the finished exports. Choose Upload to Bunny.net… after exporting. Check the file list, sizes and destination before sending anything.
  3. Start the transfer yourself. Follow progress in the upload queue. After success, copy or open the CDN link for use on your website.

A queue you can return to

Transfers have progress, cancellation and retry actions. Completed clips remain listed if another transfer fails. If the app closes, interrupted jobs wait for you to retry after relaunch; HTTP uploads restart the file from the beginning. Keep LocalioBC open while a transfer runs.

Missing or changed source files require another selection. Local exports stay on your Mac. CDN access follows your Pull Zone settings, so check those settings before sharing a link.

Local editing, optional publishing

Editing and export continue to work offline without an account. Only the finished files you explicitly confirm are uploaded directly from your Mac to your Bunny account. You can also keep exporting locally and publish the files another way.

Get the new version

The full direct-download edition is free. Existing users can choose LocalioBC → Check for Updates…; the Mac App Store edition receives its updates through Apple.
