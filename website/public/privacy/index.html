Privacy Policy

Updated November 15, 2026

Provider and scope

LocalioBC is provided by Localio Labs s.r.o. Contact: info@mylocalio.com. This policy covers the LocalioBC macOS application, this product website and support by email.

Video and audio are processed locally on macOS

LocalioBC processes imported video and audio locally using Apple system frameworks. Editing and export do not upload your media or require an account. The macOS app contains no analytics SDK, advertising identifiers or tracking SDKs. Editing and export work offline. Optional updates and user-initiated uploads, where available in your installed version, are separate from local media processing.

Files and local preferences

The sandboxed app accesses files and folders you explicitly select or drag into it. Temporary working files are used during media processing and export. Completed exports stay at the location you choose; cancelled exports may leave previously completed clips. Language and appearance preferences are stored locally on macOS. Session edits are not saved as projects.

Optional Bunny Storage uploads

Starting with version 1.3.0, direct Bunny uploads are optional. You choose the exported files and explicitly start an upload to your own Bunny Storage account. The video files, including any audio retained in the export, their remote filenames, file size, checksum and ordinary connection information are sent directly from your Mac to Bunny.net and stored in your own Bunny account. Localio Labs does not receive or store that media or your Storage Zone password on its servers. The Storage Zone password is stored in macOS Keychain; profile settings and upload queue information are stored locally so you can restart interrupted jobs. The destination uses your chosen primary Storage region; storage and replication follow your Bunny settings. CDN access follows your Pull Zone configuration and may make shared links public. Bunny storage and CDN delivery are billed separately under your Bunny account. For the service’s handling of data, see the Bunny.net Privacy Policy.

Optional application updates

Starting with version 1.2.0, the direct-download edition includes Sparkle to check for and install updates. You can opt in to daily automatic checks, enable optional automatic downloads, or choose Check for Updates… in the app menu. Automatic checks can be turned off. Your update preferences are stored locally.

Checking for updates connects to localiobc.mylocalio.com, hosted by Cloudflare, to retrieve update information and release notes. Downloading an update retrieves its package from GitHub Releases and its download infrastructure. These providers may process ordinary connection information in technical logs, including your IP address, request time and User-Agent, which can include the application and Sparkle versions. The update feature sends no system profile, clip data, media filenames or editing settings, and uploads no media files. These connections are not app usage analytics. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement for their practices.

Bunny.net affiliate links

Bunny.net is a separately paid service; it is not included with the free LocalioBC app.

Opening a link marked Affiliate sends the same public referral code for every visitor to Bunny.net in the external browser. LocalioBC does not append an app or device identifier.

Apple and external links

The Mac App Store edition does not include Sparkle; Apple manages its updates, purchases, payments and platform diagnostics under its own privacy policy. When you choose to open a web or email link, your default browser or mail app handles that action under its own policies. Such services may receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics only after you choose Allow analytics. It measures page views, including visits restored with the browser’s Back or Forward button, and clicks on application download and Bunny.net registration links. A click does not confirm a completed download, installation, registration or purchase. Google receives technical connection/browser information, the known public page path and a fixed link category and placement. Full link URLs, referral codes, URL query strings, fragments, form contents and referring-page addresses are not sent; advertising personalization is disabled. Consent and analytics cookies expire after at most 180 days. You can reject or withdraw consent at any time using Analytics preferences; this stops further collection and removes this site’s analytics cookies, without deleting reports already processed by Google. Cloudflare hosts and protects the website and processes ordinary connection logs. No third-party media is embedded. This website measurement is separate from the macOS app, which contains no analytics SDK.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your address, app/macOS version and issue description. We use it to respond and resolve the request and keep it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or private video content. Contact info@mylocalio.com to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or support practices change. The revision date appears above. No new collection or tracking is introduced without updating the relevant disclosures.
