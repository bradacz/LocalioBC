Privacy Policy

Updated November 12, 2026

Provider and scope

LocalioBC is provided by Localio Labs s.r.o. Contact: info@mylocalio.com. This policy covers the LocalioBC macOS application, this product website and support by email.

Video and audio stay on your Mac

LocalioBC processes imported video and audio locally using Apple system frameworks. The app does not upload your media, collect personal data, use analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking SDKs, or require an account. No data is sent to the developer by the app.

Files and local preferences

The sandboxed app accesses files and folders you explicitly select or drag into it. Temporary working files are used during media processing and export. Completed exports stay at the location you choose; cancelled exports may leave previously completed clips. Language and appearance preferences are stored locally on your Mac. Session edits are not saved as projects.

Apple and external links

Apple manages App Store purchases, payments and platform diagnostics under its own privacy policy. When you choose to open a web or email link, your default browser or mail app handles that action under its own policies. Such services may receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

This website

This product website does not set analytics or advertising cookies or tracking scripts. The homepage video is delivered directly by Bunny.net CDN, which receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address and request metadata to serve the video. No third-party player is embedded. The hosting provider processes ordinary connection information, including IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and protect the site. This is separate from the offline video app.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your address, app/macOS version and issue description. We use it to respond and resolve the request and keep it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or private video content. Contact info@mylocalio.com to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or support practices change. The revision date appears above. No new collection or tracking is introduced without updating the relevant disclosures.
